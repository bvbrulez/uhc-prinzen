# Rules

## Spot on Dutch tournament format

Our tournament includes two divisions to suit both competitive and social players; both run
alongside each other.
Competitive games - structured matches, proper hockey rules and a championship at stake.
Social games - The Mixed Hagelslag Cup; a fun, non-competitive cup for those who want to enjoy the
game in a relaxed, friendly environment and make new friends.

## Competition

The playing schedule is attached, but is subject to change. Matches are 25 minutes. We provide
umpires who are helping us out voluntarily; please treat them with kindness and respect.

### The Mixed Hagelslag Cup - more than just a hockey tournament; it’s a social event in disguise!

This non-competitive cup brings together mixed teams of ladies and men. Teams are randomly mixed
from three teams, so every game is a fresh combo and a chance to meet new people.
Whether you're here to show off your hockey skills or just want to have fun and maybe scout your
dance partner for later 😉- this cup is all about connection, fair play, and good vibes.

Quick and friendly rules of The Hagelslag Cup:

- Each team must include a minimum of four women (11 players total)
- Keep it safe: no high balls
- Goalkeepers optional. Goalies can also be field players if they fancy
- No referee needed - we trust in fair play
- Games last 20 minutes - just enough to break a sweat and make a new friend
